Blind Spots · What This Data Cannot Tell You
Limitations of the current dataset and analytical boundary conditions.
Units Without a Tracker: If a completed unit never submitted a CSV tracker, its issues are invisible to the learning curve. Currently 0 of 4 completed units are missing trackers in this prototype.
Uncategorised Rows: 0% of imported rows could not be mapped to the canonical taxonomy. Any unmapped rows are flagged during import but excluded from repeat-issue analysis.
People Data Aggregated Only: Training completion and shift coverage are reported at unit or shift level only. No individual worker names or records are stored. Frontline perspectives and informal workarounds are not systematically captured.
Lessons Limited to This Client Programme: The learning curve draws only from Northstar Logistics' own 4 completed DCs. Cross-client sharing of go-live patterns would require data anonymisation and explicit client approval, which is out of scope for this prototype.
Informal Workarounds Unrecorded: Ad-hoc fixes applied by site teams without logging (e.g. manually re-labelling pallets, or swapping scanner firmware overnight) do not appear in any tracker and therefore cannot inform future kickoff packs.

Why It Is Designed This Way (Context from Managing Director Interviews)
1. Fixed-Fee Business Model

Miebach projects are primarily fixed fee. Extra coordination hours and go-live delays directly erode consulting margin. LaunchLoop protects PM bandwidth and shields delivery dates.

2. Adoption Decides Digital Success

Site teams resist heavy enterprise software and prefer local spreadsheets. LaunchLoop meets teams where they are by ingesting existing trackers rather than forcing replacement.

3. Human-in-the-Loop Deliverables

AI and algorithms assist in analysis and drafting; consultants validate every client-facing deliverable. Nothing is presented to the client without PM sign-off.

4. Multi-Site Centralised Consistency

Clients with centralised supply chain directors value predictable, cross-site repeatability. Repeat-issue learning curves institutionalise knowledge across successive launches.
